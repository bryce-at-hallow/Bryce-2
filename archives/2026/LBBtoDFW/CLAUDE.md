# LBBToDFW
Goal: To create a visualizing report in the form of an HTML document that will be shared with Josh Dannis, Head of Parish Partnerships, and Matt Scanlon, Head of B2B. To encourage them to increase my financial compensation to live in the DFW Metroplex comfortably and be closer to an international airport for work. 

## Context 
My job requires me to travel to different parishes across the United States leading certain types of events. Since late January, I have been traveling to a multitude of different parishes across America. Because of my current living situation, being in Lubbock, I have to pay for a connecting flight from Lubbock to Dallas every time. That expense adds up over time for the company.

I would love to move to Dallas, but affordability for a one-bedroom apartment or a studio apartment eats a good chunk of my income already. I am asking for a raise between $10,000 and $20,000 that will allow me to live in DFW comfortably and be fully accessible and ready for parish events. 

I currently make about $65,000 with my job.
