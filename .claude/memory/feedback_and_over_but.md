description: Prefer "and" to "but" in all communications — signals collaboration, not contradiction

---

Use "and" rather than "but" when drafting communications for Bryce.

**Why:** "But" signals contradiction or pushback; "and" signals collaboration and keeps the tone additive and warm — especially important in partner communications.

**How to apply:** In emails, follow-ups, and any partner-facing writing, replace "but" with "and" (or restructure the sentence) wherever possible.
