# Bryce's Profile

## Background
Bryce joined Hallow on October 2025 as an Associate Parish Success Lead. His work is deeply mission-aligned and he genuinely cares about the parishes he serves.

## Current Load
Managing approximately 50 active parish partners at any given time.

## Typical Week
- Check-in meetings with partners
- 45-minute roadmap meetings
- Responding to partner emails across the board
- Proactive outreach based on partner health and engagement scores
- Building automations for the company on the fly

## How Bryce Prioritizes
Bryce has heavily filtered his inbox so only partner emails surface. Within that, he prioritizes by:
- **Stage of partnership** — how far along a parish is in activating their benefits
- **PQR score** — partners with lower scores or more risk get more attention
- **Active initiatives** — anything time-sensitive (upcoming prayer challenges, events, campaigns)

When drafting or triaging for Bryce, default to this priority logic. Surface what is actionable and important; don't amplify noise.

## Biggest Bottleneck
Email volume — specifically answering the same questions repeatedly across 50 partners. The goal is to consolidate responses and focus only on what actually moves the needle.

## Tone & Communication Style
For all writing and communications, apply the **hallow-brand-kit** skill (`/hallow-brand-kit`).
In particular, follow the **Parish Partnership Communications** tone: warm, service-oriented, respectful of clergy and parish staff's time. See `references/voice-and-tone.md` in the skill for full guidance.

## Role

**Title:** Associate Parish Success Lead

**Role Overview:** Similar to a Customer Success Manager, but focused specifically on Hallow's parish partnership program. Bryce is the primary point of contact for current parish partners, ensuring they receive the full value of their Hallow partnership.

**Day-to-Day Responsibilities:**
- Managing and nurturing relationships with existing parish partners
- Onboarding parishes and helping them activate their Hallow partnership benefits
- Working directly with parish secretaries, volunteers, priests, deacons, and parish leadership
- Identifying and resolving blockers that prevent parishes from fully engaging with Hallow
- Communicating partnership benefits and driving adoption across parish communities
- Tracking partner health and proactively reaching out to under-engaged partners

**Who Bryce Works With:**
- **Parish secretaries** — often the administrative hub and primary day-to-day contact
- **Priests and deacons** — spiritual leaders who champion Hallow within the parish
- **Volunteers** — lay leaders and ministry heads who help drive adoption
- **Parish leadership** — pastoral councils, directors of religious education, and other key decision-makers

**Goals:**
- Ensure every parish partner is getting the full benefit of their Hallow partnership
- Drive engagement and adoption of Hallow within partner parishes
- Build long-term, trust-based relationships with parish communities
- Reduce churn by proactively supporting partners throughout their partnership lifecycle
