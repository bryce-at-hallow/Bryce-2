# Hallow

## About Hallow
Hallow is the #1 app for Catholic and Christian prayer, meditation, Bible, and sleep. Hallow's mission is to help people pray and deepen their relationship with Jesus Christ, rooted in the sacraments and centered on the Eucharist.

Notable stats:
- 1.4+ billion prayers prayed through the app
- 330,000+ five-star reviews
- 10,000+ prayer sessions, guides, and music options
- Active campaigns like Lent Pray40 with 1.9M+ participants

Notable contributors include Mark Wahlberg, Chris Pratt, and Jonathan Roumie.

## B2B Team (Bryce's Team)

### Parish Partnership Program
Hallow's parish partnership program connects Catholic parishes with the app to help their communities pray together. Bryce serves as the primary point of contact for current parish partners.

**Partnership Tiers:** Hallow offers three tiers of parish partnership. See [`context/partnership-features.md`](partnership-features.md) for the full feature breakdown.

### Territory Model 
The B2B team operates on a **territorial model** with four regions. Bryce is assigned the **Midwest territory**, though he may also manage partners from other regions. 

| Region | States |
|--------|--------|
| **Midwest** *(Bryce's territory)* | ND, SD, NE, KS, MN, IA, MO, IL, WI, IN, OH, KY, TN |
| **Northeast** | MI, WV, VA, DC, MD, DE, NJ, RI, CT, MA, ME, NH, VT, NY, PA |
| **South** | TX, OK, AR, LA, MS, AL, GA, SC, NC, FL |
| **West** | WA, OR, ID, MT, WY, CO, NM, UT, AZ, NV, CA, AK, HI |

*Note: Michigan is in Northeast (not traditional Midwest); Kentucky and Tennessee are in Midwest (not traditional South).*
