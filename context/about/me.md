# Bryce McWhirter

## Who Bryce Is
**Title:** Associate Parish Success Lead
**Started:** October 2025
**Territory:** Midwest (see [`hallow.md`](hallow.md) for full territory map)
**Team:** B2B / Parish Partnerships — see [`team.md`](team.md) for team contacts

Bryce is deeply mission-aligned and genuinely cares about the parishes he serves. His work sits at the intersection of customer success, relationship management, and Catholic community building. He is the primary point of contact for current parish partners, ensuring they receive the full value of their Hallow partnership.

**Who he works with:** Parish secretaries (primary day-to-day contact), priests and deacons, volunteers, and parish leadership (pastoral councils, directors of religious education).

---

## His Work

**Current Load:** ~50 active parish partners at any given time.

**Typical Week:**
- Check-in meetings with partners
- 45-minute roadmap meetings
- Responding to partner emails
- Proactive outreach based on partner health and engagement signals
- Building automations for the team

**How He Prioritizes:**
1. **Stage of partnership** — how far along a parish is in activating their benefits
2. **PQR score** — lower scores or higher risk get more attention (see [`program/pqr-scoring.md`](../program/pqr-scoring.md))
3. **Active initiatives** — anything time-sensitive (prayer challenges, events, campaigns)

**Biggest Bottleneck:** Email volume — answering the same questions repeatedly across 50 partners. The goal is to consolidate responses and focus only on what actually moves the needle.

## How to Work With Bryce

- **Optimize for actionability.** Surface what is important and ready to act on; don't amplify noise.
- **Be concise.** Bryce's time is split across 50 partners — brevity is a feature.
- **Tone:** Warm, service-oriented, respectful of clergy and parish staff's time. Apply the **hallow-brand-kit** skill for all partner-facing writing.
