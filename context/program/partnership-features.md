## Tier Overview

Hallow offers three partnership tiers: **Basic**, **Growth**, and **Expansion**.

**Key notes:**
- Basic is not available for parishes with fewer than 1,000 parishioners
- Hallow no longer sells 1-year contracts — minimum is 2 years

## Feature Descriptions

### Parish Page & the App
All parishioners — premium and free — can join the parish page. Features include prayer challenges, a community feed, small groups, prayer intentions, and a parish chat. Parish page admins can manage members, customize the page, and access a community dashboard.

**Church Tab — Community Features**
The Church Tab is the primary space where parishes engage their community. Parishioners can:
- Participate in parish prayer challenges together
- Access content in the community library
- Post and pray for each other's prayer requests
- Share reflections and introduction posts

> By default, prayers related to mental health are hidden from the community feed. No admin action is needed — this is the system default.

**Church Tab — Admin Capabilities**
Parish page admins can manage the community directly in the app:
- Add or remove content from the featured section (max 3 items at a time)
- Set a custom profile image, name, and bio for the parish page
- Customize the member welcome message and icebreaker prompts
- Assign or remove community admins
- Remove members from the community
- Report and remove prayer requests

See [`playbooks/church-tab-admin-setup.md`](../playbooks/church-tab-admin-setup.md) for step-by-step instructions on each.

### Small Groups
Available on all tiers. Any parishioner can create a small group (think: mini parish pages). The group creator becomes an admin. Small groups can have up to 5 devotionals. The parish page admin can remove small groups.

### Prayer Challenges
Guided prayer journeys (novenas, seasonal collections, at-home retreats) that run on the parish page. Parish Leads schedule these via Tool and can make premium content free for parishioners. Challenges remain on the parish page for the duration + 7 days.
- **Basic:** 2/year
- **Growth:** 5/year
- **Expansion:** 8/year

**Types:**
- **Global Challenges** — happen app-wide at the same time (Advent, Lent, Easter)
- **Challenges** — curated collections (How to Pray, Saints Challenge, etc.)
- **Dailies** — parishes can have up to 2 dailies on their page (Daily Mass Prep counts as free)

### Giving Campaigns
An opportunity for parishes to prayerfully raise funds for a project of their choosing. Giving Campaigns appear in the "Up Next" section on the parish page and are paired with a prayer challenge (e.g., Surrender Novena). Unused Giving Campaign slots can be used as regular prayer challenges.
- **Basic:** ❌
- **Growth:** 1/year (counts as one of the 5 prayer challenges)
- **Expansion:** 2/year (counts as two of the 8 prayer challenges)

### Pastor Audio
A short audio recording (2–4 minutes) by the pastor, featured on the parish page for 7 days. Used to welcome parishioners or introduce an upcoming prayer challenge or giving campaign. Parish provides an MP4 file at least 4–6 weeks in advance.
- **Basic:** ❌
- **Growth:** 1/year
- **Expansion:** 2/year

### Growth Event
A Hallow team member visits the parish to cast vision for building a culture of prayer. Includes one speaker (contract speaker preferred), one presentation/talk, and can include Mass announcements. Available for Growth parishes only.
- **Basic:** ❌
- **Growth:** ✅ (1 in-person event/year)
- **Expansion:** ❌

### Complimentary Subscriptions
Full Hallow Premium subscriptions the parish can distribute to staff, catechists, ministry leaders, OCIA participants, volunteers, or anyone they choose. Distributed via a single promo code.
- **Basic:** 25
- **Growth:** 50
- **Expansion:** 75

> Note: Legacy materials may reference "discretionary" or "complementary" accounts — these are now unified under "Subscriptions."

### Partner Heralds
Parish staff, volunteers, or Hallow advocates who help implement the partnership at the parish level. Roles include: Parish Page Admin, Small Group Admin, Prayer Warrior, and Hallow Advocate. Supported by Hallow via virtual webinars.

### Collateral
Promotional and physical materials to support parish-wide adoption and awareness.

### Marketing Campaign
A Hallow-supported marketing push to recruit new parishioners to the parish page. Should only be used after the parish reaches 10% activation and has daily engagement. Best timing: January or August. Not recommended during Advent or Lent.
- **Basic:** ❌
- **Growth:** 1/year
- **Expansion:** 2/year

### Contract Speakers
Hallow hires or contracts qualified Catholic speakers to present at parish events. Available for Growth and Expansion tiers. Preferred for Growth events; Parish Leads may also attend Expansion missions.
- **Basic:** ❌
- **Growth:** ✅
- **Expansion:** ✅

### Mission Event
A 3-night parish mission from a Hallow team member or contract speaker. Up to 4 presentations (3 for the whole parish + 1 for staff). Casts a vision for building a culture of prayer in the parish.
- **Basic:** ❌
- **Growth:** ❌
- **Expansion:** ✅

### Parishioner Discounts
All tiers provide discounts for parishioners purchasing Hallow Premium. Applied via promo codes at `hallow.com/parish`.

| Tier | New Subscribers | Renewing Subscribers |
|---|---|---|
| Basic | 6-month free trial | N/A |
| Growth | 6-month free trial + 25% off | 25% off |
| Expansion | 6-month free trial + 50% off | 50% off |

**Parish Discount**
The Parish discount code is offered through the Church page. Free subscribers will see a button that says 'Claim Subscription' in purple with a gift icon. When parishioners click that button, they will see their unique offer in the app.

> Accounts that were previously subscribers only qualify for the ongoing discount (25% or 50%), not the free trial.
