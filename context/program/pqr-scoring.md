# Partner Quality Rating (PQR) — Scoring Rubric

The PQR score (1–10) measures the health and depth of a parish partnership. Assign a score on first impressions, then update it as the relationship develops.

---

| Score | Label | Description |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Failing | No engagement, constant frustration, no goal alignment. Churn is imminent. |
| 2 | Unstable | Poor communication, unmet expectations, little trust. Partner is actively considering alternatives. |
| 3 | Struggling | Minimal engagement with frequent challenges. Improvement efforts feel one-sided. |
| 4 | Weak | Transactional partnership, limited collaboration. Partner is not satisfied but hasn't disengaged. |
| 5 | Neutral | Functional but lacks depth. Partner interacts when necessary but is not invested. |
| 6 | Developing | Some positive engagement, foundation of trust, but stronger alignment needed. |
| 7 | Engaged | Partner sees value, collaborates fairly well. Regular interactions with room for optimization. |
| 8 | Strong | Trust-driven partnership with mutual benefits. Partner views success lead as a valuable resource. |
| 9 | Thriving | High engagement, proactive collaboration, aligned goals. Partner sees partnership as essential. |
| 10 | Ideal Partner | Deeply strategic, long-term partnership. Partner advocates for Hallow and sees it as critical to their growth. |

---

## How to Use This Score

- **Initial score:** Assign based on first impressions during onboarding (tone, enthusiasm, team capacity).
- **Ongoing updates:** Revisit after each significant interaction. Log the reason for any score change in the partner's Relationship History.
- **Action thresholds:**
  - **7–10:** Maintain and deepen. Look for ways to activate advanced tier features.
  - **5–6:** Invest intentionally. Identify what's blocking deeper engagement.
  - **1–4:** Triage. Understand the root issue before investing more time. Flag for leadership if churn risk is real.
